# Mzansi Votes — Product Requirements (condensed)
**Vision:** Know Your Area. Know Your Leaders. Know Your Vote. A nonpartisan, source-linked civic platform for all nine provinces.
**Users:** first-time voters, older/low-literacy users, low-end Android on slow networks, journalists/researchers, civil society.
**Core questions answered:** my ward/municipality; my councillor, mayor and governing party/coalition; who contests; past and current results; what changed; service delivery & finances; promises vs delivery; where/how to vote; how to report problems.
**Non-negotiables:** (1) distinguish fact / provisional / projection / allegation; (2) never fabricate data — unavailable stays unavailable; (3) official declarations supersede provisional; (4) no governing label before council confirmation; (5) address ≠ registration; (6) POPIA data minimisation, no ballot-choice data; (7) WCAG 2.2 AA, low-data first; (8) auditable admin changes.
**Success criteria:** the 12 acceptance criteria in the master brief; Phase-1 slice status in `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` §3.
**Design:** deep navy surfaces, off-white content, teal accent, semantic status colours; party colours only in charts with pattern fallbacks; 44px targets; skip link; reduced-motion; dark mode.
**Risks:** official data access (IEC/MDB), boundary-version accuracy, translation/legal review, moderation load, political-neutrality perception → transparent methodology + corrections channel.
